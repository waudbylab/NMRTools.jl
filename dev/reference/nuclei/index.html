Nuclei, coherences and Xi ratios

Nuclei

NMRTools defines the nuclei below as instances of Nucleus. Their spin quantum numbers, gyromagnetic ratios and Xi ratios are returned by spin, gyromagneticratio and xi_ratio. A nucleus can also be looked up from a string with nucleus, e.g. nucleus("15N").

using NMRTools, Markdown
fmt(x) = isnothing(x) ? "–" : string(x)
rows = ["| `$n` | $(fmt(spin(n))) | $(fmt(gyromagneticratio(n))) | $(fmt(xi_ratio(n; aqueous=true))) | $(fmt(xi_ratio(n; aqueous=false))) |"
        for n in instances(Nucleus)]
Markdown.parse("""
| Nucleus | Spin | γ (rad s⁻¹ T⁻¹) | Xi ratio (DSS, aqueous) | Xi ratio (TMS, organic) |
|:--------|:-----|:----------------|:------------------------|:------------------------|
$(join(rows, "\n"))
""")

Xi ratios are the IUPAC ratios of each nucleus's reference frequency to that of ¹H in the reference compound, used for indirect referencing (see Reference chemical shifts). DSS is the reference for aqueous samples and TMS for organic solvents.

Coherences

Coherences are described by SQ (single quantum, on one nucleus) and MQ (multiple quantum, a combination of nuclei with coherence orders), both subtypes of Coherence. coherenceorder gives the total order:

coherenceorder(SQ(H1)), coherenceorder(MQ(((H1, 1), (C13, 1)))), coherenceorder(MQ(((H1, 1), (C13, -1))))
(1, 2, 0)
